## Quick Revision: NumPy Operators

| Operator | Meaning |
|---|---|
| > | Greater than |
| < | Less than |
| == | Equal to |
| != | Not equal to |
| >= | Greater than or equal to |
| <= | Less than or equal to |
| & | Both conditions (AND) |
| \| | Either condition (OR) |
